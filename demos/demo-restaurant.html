Our story

From a single boat to Bahrain's table

Bayt Al Bahar began in 2014 with one family, one fishing boat, and a stubborn belief that the Gulf's catch deserved better than the freezer. Today we still buy from the same harbour every morning, still grill over charcoal, and still seat you like family. The sea gives; we simply cook it honestly.
