Our story

Three generations of noses

Amara began in a small Muharraq souq shop where our grandfather aged oud in clay pots and blended attars by memory. Three generations later we still blend by hand, still age slowly, and still believe a fragrance should be personal. Every bottle is mixed in small batches - never rushed, never mass-produced.
